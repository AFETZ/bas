# Project Instructions

## Scientific Papers

Before drafting, revising, or auditing any scientific paper in this repository,
read and follow `revised_scientific_paper_workflow_stepanyants.md`.

Treat the workflow as the default project standard:

- verified facts and artifacts precede manuscript text;
- every numerical result requires provenance;
- every claim requires evidence and an explicit limitation boundary;
- terminology and metrics must remain consistent across the full manuscript;
- Introduction, Results, Discussion, and Conclusion must make claims at the same
  strength;
- simulation evidence must not be presented as field validation;
- AI may assist with structure, rewriting, and audits, but must not invent facts,
  results, citations, figures, novelty, or scientific decisions.

For UAS/BAS papers, apply the workflow's BAS and digital-twin addendum, especially
its forbidden-overclaim rules.
